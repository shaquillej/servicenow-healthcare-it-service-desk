| Troubleshooting: Printer Not Printing from Exam Room Workstations | KB0010002 | Common causes and fixes for exam room print failures, with hardware escalation path |
| VPN Setup and Troubleshooting for Remote/Satellite Clinic Staff | KB0010003 | Initial VPN provisioning plus fixes for connection drops, MFA push issues, and unreachable EHR over VPN |
| Badge Reader Not Working - Troubleshooting Steps | KB0010004 | Diagnosing badge sync delays, access-level gaps, and physical badge/reader hardware faults |

## Deflection Value

Four of the twelve simulated tickets (INC0010003, INC0010007, INC0010009, INC0010012) map directly to a published knowledge article, illustrating how a well-maintained knowledge base reduces repeat Tier 1 volume and speeds up resolution when a ticket is still needed.
